Who I Am

About Me

I'm a third-year B.Tech student specialising in Computer Science and Engineering (Data Science) at JSS Academy of Technical Education, Noida. My academic journey is driven by genuine curiosity for how software systems are designed, built and optimised.

I spend a significant portion of my time solving algorithmic problems on platforms like LeetCode, CodeChef and Codeforces. Competitive programming sharpens my logical reasoning and teaches me to think clearly under constraints — skills I carry directly into software development.

On the development side, I built MediKiosk — a full-stack medical interview platform that handles patient screening, OCR-based document extraction, AI-assisted question selection and a doctor-facing dashboard. It's the kind of project where each layer taught me something new.

I learn best by building things, breaking them and understanding why they broke. My goal is to become a strong software engineer — one who writes clean, maintainable code and can navigate both algorithmic problems and real-world engineering challenges.
